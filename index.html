CalcuBlock: Block Calculator

A colourful calculator built from toy blocks, with a fun math game: solve equations, build streaks and collect star coins.

Support

Found a bug, have an idea, or need help? Email pavelpanasenco@gmail.com and we'll get back to you.

How the game works

  • Tap GAME to get a question, type the answer and press =.
  • Solve 10 in a row to earn a star coin.
  • One star coin fades away each day, so come back and play!
  • Tap CALC to go back to the calculator.

Privacy

CalcuBlock collects no personal information and shows only family-friendly, non-personalized ads. Read the full privacy policy.
