Privacy Policy

CalcuBlock: Block Calculator · Effective 16 October 2026

CalcuBlock is a calculator and math game for all ages, made by Pavel Panasenco ("we"). This policy explains what information the app handles. In short: we do not collect any personal information, and the only third party in the app is Google AdMob, which shows family-friendly ads.

Information we collect

None. CalcuBlock has no accounts, no sign-up, no contact forms and no analytics of our own. We never ask for a name, email address, photo, contacts or location.

Information stored on the device

The app saves two small values on the device itself so the game works between sessions:

  • the number of star coins earned in game mode, and
  • the date the app last checked for the daily star-coin loss.

These never leave the device and are not sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Advertising

CalcuBlock shows a banner ad provided by Google AdMob. Because the app may be used by children, every ad request is configured for child treatment, whatever the user's age:

  • ads are tagged as child-directed under the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and as intended for users under the age of consent under the GDPR and UK GDPR;
  • only ads rated G (General Audiences) are allowed;
  • ads are not personalized and are not based on the user's past behaviour;
  • the app never asks for permission to track and does not access the device's advertising identifier (IDFA).

To deliver and measure ads, Google may process limited technical information such as the device's IP address (which can indicate an approximate location), device model and operating system, and how ads are displayed and tapped. Google uses this to serve the ad, prevent fraud and count impressions, under its own policies for child-directed requests. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's guidance on child-directed treatment.

Tapping an ad may open a web page or the App Store outside CalcuBlock. We recommend that a parent is present when children use apps that show ads.

Children's privacy

CalcuBlock is suitable for children and complies with COPPA and the GDPR as described above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe information about your child has been collected through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your choices

  • You can delete the app at any time; this removes everything it stored on the device.
  • iPhone users can limit ad tracking for all apps in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. CalcuBlock does not track, regardless of this setting.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect.
